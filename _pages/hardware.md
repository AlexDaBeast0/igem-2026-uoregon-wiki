## Targets: Water Wells & Agriculture Runoff

Based on our literature review and interviews with experts, we concluded that it would be best to tailor our solution to two different waste streams. Well water nitrification has local implications and addressing this issue would directly benefit the lives of well owners and our local community. To make an impact at a larger scale, we also considered how our system would fit to reduce nitrogen pollution in agriculture runoff.

The goal of our AquaFlock product is to have it fit in a homeowner's well system. This allows residents to limit the amount of nitrogen in their water and reduce negative health impacts that come with nitrogen pollution. We wanted our design to be easy to instal and fit within the established well system. Our system will be installed after the well pump and before the pressure tank (water tank). This allows our system to be close to the surface and easier to maintain. It also allows us to better control the flow rate of the water going through our system which will help our bacteria thrive. Our AquaFlock solution would allow homeowners to fertilize their gardens with the flocculated bacteria which would limit the need of fertilizer and reduce environmental consequences.

To make a bigger impact, our team also looked at targeting nitrogen removal in agriculture runoff, a larger contributor to contamination. Implementing our AquaFlock product in the subsurface drainage system would be the ideal way to do this. The subsurface drainage infrastructure collects the groundwater into a pipe. Targeting these pipes allows us to stop contamination before it leaches into the groundwater. Woodchip bioreactors are implemented in this way and are currently used as a solution to this issue. One of the benefits of the AquaFlock bacteria, is the ability to harvest nitrogen-rich bacteria from the system itself. Our system would be used in place of a woodchip bioreactor and include a way to extract the flocculated bacteria so that it could be reused as fertilizer to reduce nitrogen pollution.
