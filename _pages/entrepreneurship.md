Early in project development, we decided that we wanted to address the *lack of viable nitrate water purification systems on the market*. As a team we decided to compete in the *Oregon Innovation Challenge* hosted by Universtiy of Oregon’s Center for Entrepreneushrip within the Lundquist College of Businesss to apply the findings of our research early on. Over the course of 5 months, we went through multiple design iterations and honed our scientific communication techniques to convey complex sythetic biology to a general audience. We worked closely with SheerID ex-CEO
