Recapturing Nitrate Pollution

Modern agriculture is characterized by heavy use of nitrogen fertilizer, creating a major source of nitrogen pollution. Almost half of the fertilizer is not absorbed by plants, instead leaching into surface and ground water. Excess nitrogen has large-scale, catastrophic impacts on both the natural environment and human communities. We propose a bacteria-based solution to recapture nitrogen from water. Engineered bacteria uptake environmental nitrogen as nitrate, which triggers expression of recombinant cyanophycin synthetase. The enzyme assembles nitrogen-rich amino acids into the storage biopolymer cyanophycin. Once cyanophycin has accumulated, a second genetic circuit is triggered to cause bacterial flocculation, allowing collection of the nitrogen-rich culture. The collected cells may then be sterilized and used as fertilizer. Implemented in nitrogen-polluted water streams, our system provides an element of circularization to the agricultural fertilizer industry while simultaneously addressing a significant source of pollution.
