**Our Values**

As a team, we acknowledge the multitude of life experiences, ideas, perspectives, and values we each bring to the table. As we worked to form what has become a very meaningful project for our team, certain care was taken into consideration to recognize each unique and individual passion of every member of the team. First and foremost, it was a priority to create space for the good work of each member, whether that came in the form of research, lab work, or outreach. Every aspect of our project was lovingly tended, and communication was always held as an utmost non-negotiable. Secondly, we all believe deeply that science is a tool that can be used to bring out the best in people, and to help us all move towards and build the world we want to live in. For this reason, it became very important for our work as a team to contribute to the world of education and increase the accessibility of information regarding synthetic biology and environmental health (link education tab, and maybe survey?). And, in line with the desire to build the world we all would be proud to be a part of, we worked to emphasize four of the United Nations Sustainable 17 Development Goals . The goals we felt best fit our project and were therefore elected as focal areas include: #3 Good Health, #4 Quality Education, #6 Clean Water and Sanitation, and #12 Responsible Consumption and Production.
