# Step 1: Implementation in the Home

Our primary focus is the drawdown of nitrates in well water, which is the waste stream that most impacts human health link. With the use of our product, we anticipate a marked reduction of nitrates in well water, returning the water to a safe drinking standard. 

# Step 2: Implementation at the Source

Our second focal area involves the implementation of our product at the source of nitrate pollution: agriculture. As the water filters through the soil or runs off of land used for agriculture, the water collects nitrate from the ammonium fertilizer that is commonly used to fertilize plants. By upscaling and reinforcing our product to be used in this more extreme and potentially volatile environment, we hope to work to cut off the pollution at the source, reducing the need for water filtration down the line. 

# Step 3: Harvest

As our bacteria work to uptake nitrate from the polluted water—whether at the agricultural or well water waste streams—they will eventually become so full of branched-chain, nitrogen-rich proteins that they will begin to flocculate and sink to the bottom of our AquaFlock hardware system link. We will have the ability to harvest the bacteria, which, as a result of their nitrate-uptake function, have become outstanding candidates to serve as a fertilizer alternative for farmers in the area. 

# Step 4: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

As the process of uptake and reapplication continues, our goal is to close the loop on fertilizer usage and localize accessibility to the AquaFlock fertilizer product itself. In our ‘perfect world’, well owners and farmers would be able to use our hardware and bacteria to uptake nitrates out of the water, effectively turning all nitrates back into usable fertilizer, to then be reapplied and reabsorbed by the AquaFlock system if nitrate levels continued to remain in an unsafe range. This cycle would effectively continue until water nitrates reached such a point in which our product was no longer of use, in which case, our ultimate goal will be accomplished. 

# Our values

As a team, we acknowledge the multitude of life experiences, ideas, perspectives, and values we each bring to the table. As we worked to form what has become a very meaningful project for our team, certain care was taken into consideration to recognize each unique and individual passion of every member of the team. First and foremost, it was a priority to create space for the good work of each member, whether that came in the form of research, lab work, or outreach. Every aspect of our project was lovingly tended, and communication was always held as an utmost non-negotiable. 

Secondly, we all believe deeply that science is a tool that can be used to bring out the best in people, and to help us all move towards and build the world we want to live in. For this reason, it became very important for our work as a team to [contribute to the world of education](/uoregon/edu-events) and increase the accessibility of information regarding synthetic biology and environmental health. And, in line with the desire to build the world we all would be proud to be a part of, we worked to emphasize four of the United Nations Sustainable 17 Development Goals. The [goals we felt best fit our project](/uoregon/sustainability) and were therefore elected as focal areas include: #3 Good Health, #4 Quality Education, #6 Clean Water and Sanitation, and #12 Responsible Consumption and Production.
