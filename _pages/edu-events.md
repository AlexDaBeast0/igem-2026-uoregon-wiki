# Educational Outreach Events

For wiki reviewers, photos from each event can be previewed here: 

https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wCMQG46riQ0E64h-JwlaEVAttzknpsmT?usp=sharing

To tailor our educational resources to diverse knowledge levels, we followed the DBTL framework to develop and present three differentiated synthetic biology workshops. 

We emphasized two interconnected teaching principles: STEAM through illustrated workbooks, and UDL (Universal Design for Learning) through scaffolded curriculum design. More information about this can be read at HYPERLINK TO PEDAGOGY & TEACHING PHILOSOPHY.

## Workshop 1: Intro to Synthetic Biology

We developed our first workshop: “Workshop 1: Intro to Synthetic Biology”. This was a one-hour workshop paired with a worksheet for high school students with minimal science background and mixed career interests.

## Workshop 2: Engineering SynBio Solutions

Informed by the results of our first event, we learned what elements to continue highlighting (illustrated worksheets) and what we could do to lower the barrier to learning about an interdisciplinary field like synthetic biology. Thus, we designed our second workshop. For this event, our audience was high school students with minimal science background and an interest in studying science.
