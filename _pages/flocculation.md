Outline:

- Intro: what role the module serves in the bacteria/project, what are its requirements and specifications.

Methods for harvesting the bacteria after nitrate uptake were essential to the project, as a key component was the production of a renewable nitrogen fertilizer. In order for this to be possible, we needed a way to collect only the engineered Escherichia coli storing the maximum amount of our desired nitrogen; leaving the rest to retain the population while continuing to uptake nitrates. Working within a filtration system context, there are a lot of possibilities as to how we could achieve this, but the one we landed on was inducible flocculation. This would allow the bacteria to clump together when receiving a signal, ideally nitrate levels, but in this case IPTG. Flocculation is an advantageous method, as sedimentation filtration is already quite common, and the flocculation makes the bacteria sink.
